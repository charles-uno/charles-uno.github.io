

## cedh could be a real format

wotc could maintain it as a turn-3 format. a bit faster than modern. ban LED, mana crypt, mana vault. shut down the turn-1 combo nonsense

upside:
- make non-blue decks viable
- competitive play gets cheaper, more accessible
- top-tier play gets more diverse
- bracket system can stay how it is. bracket 4 is modern poert level. bracket 3 is standard. etc

downside: 
- some old cards get orphaned
- disruptive

## commander would be better without tutors

not just [[demonic tutor]]. anything that allows you to search and shuffle. possible exception for tutoring *basic* lands. 

searching a 99-card deck for a singleton is a huge waste of everyone's time
