a grain of sand in the desert doesn't have any agency. it moves according to the forces that act on it. it may be blown into the air by the wind, then pulled back to earth by gravity. it may collide with other grains in a sandstorm. it may get stuck to the foot of a lizard. but ultimately, whatever path is takes, it follows the laws of physics

this doesn't mean we can perfectly predict the path of every grain of sand. we can only measure so much

things can also get weird when we zoom out. a lot of ink has been spilled studying the movement of sand dunes across the desert. the math gets really complicated. this is an emergent property that is *not* obvious from looking at the fundamental laws for a single grain of sand















Atoms don't go rogue

Everything is made of fundamental particles

Fundamental particles behave according to very strict rules

This is easy to understand when we look at a rock. You can calculate the exact trajectory of a rock when it is dropped or thrown. This is true even if you're throwing it into a hurricane, though you would need a lot of details about the starting state

This is how weather models work! Air pressure temperature, etc. They all progress according to well-behaved rules. Predictions are only limited by (1) the granularity of our measurement, (2) computer power to crunch all the numbers. But in principle, it's all knowable

Not technically deterministic. Probabilistic. Just barely. Quantum mechanics. Not generally visible at the everyday level

This is easy to accept when talking about clouds or rocks. We are made of the same stuff. The atoms that make you up follow the exact same rules as the atoms that compose a rock. Still no room to have opinions or go rogue

Zooming out a minute: wiggle your fingers. 
wiggle is caused by muscles contracting in your wrist
muscles contract based on signals from your brain (powered by fuel in carried by your blood)
That signal can be traced back to the brain
But it doesn't start there by magic!
Your brain is made of neurons sending signals back and forth
Neurons processing input from your eyes
Neurons sending signals to your heart to keep pumping
Neurons sending signals to your arm
The brain is very complicated, but it's not magic
Neurons are made of atoms
They follow the same rules
Your eyes see pixels on a screen, send that signal to your brain
decode it as "wiggle your fingers"
run that through a bunch of processing
eventually send a signal down your arm (or not)

Computer made of meat
Architecturally very different from a "real" computer
Your phone or laptop has a single CPU, or maybe a handful of them
That's where all the processing happens
Data is stored elsewhere
Your brain doesn't have a single central processing unit

But your brain, like a computer, is a complicated machine
It takes inputs
Processes them according to rigid rules
And produces outputs/behaviors

So what is consciousness?
Good question!
Maybe it's an emergent by-product
A passenger in your brain
Maybe it's an illusion, and you are the only conscious thing, and everyone else isn't
But it's certainly not driving
There is no mechanism for a choice made by your consciousness to change the behavior of cells and atoms
Instead, your computer-brain is churning inputs into outputs
And your consciousness is a passenger, making sense of the computation as it happens
