Companion!

they did a power level errata. The first in a long time. This was not an issue of fixing a corner case, like with cascade and split cards. They rewrote the mechanic to be entirely different. the text on the first printing of companions was no longer dated, but *wrong*

what is important to commander?
- building around your commander
- the rest of the deck is high-variance to balance it out

lutri! sees fringe Modern play. fun and interesting

Lurrus was the worst. actually *reduced* the variance of your sixty. smaller available card pool

shuffling a 99-card deck is a waste of everyone's time

you may personally like playing cards that do this, or decks that rely on it, but nobody likes the actual tutoring

if we banned tutors, we could probably unban other stuff. there are a bunch of cards on the ban list that are miserable when you see them all the time. if they just showed up once in a while, there's a decent chance they would be ok
- dockside
- griselbrand
- emrakul
- sundering titan
- iona
- etc
